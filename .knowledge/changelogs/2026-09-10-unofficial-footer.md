# Footer saying the tool is unofficial

**Date:** 2026-09-10

## What changed

`SiteFooter` on both screens: one plain sentence saying the tool was made by a Neo
Games player and has no tie to the server.

## Why

Asked for. A page that carries the server's name, palette and item names can be
mistaken for the server's own tool.

## Notes

The disclaimer doubles as the privacy statement, and that is deliberate: "não pede
login e não se conecta a lugar nenhum, só lê o arquivo que já está no seu PC" is both
the reason it is not official and the reason it is safe to use. Two claims that would
each be a separate paragraph read better as one.

It ships on the upload screen too, not only the dashboard — that is where someone who
does not know what the site is actually lands.

Three things went in and came back out at the author's call: a gold "Projeto de
jogador, não é oficial" pill, a line about the names belonging to their owners, and
the inline emphasis on "não tem nenhum vínculo com o servidor". The footer is one
unstyled paragraph now.
